✦How to use Word Craft
Learn a word

Tap the card to reveal its meaning. Tap Deep Dive for examples, memory hooks, and context.

Move through cards

Phone: swipe left for the next card or right to go back.

Computer: drag the card, use the Back/Next buttons, or press ← →.

Quick Test

Choose an answer or press 1–4. A wrong answer returns for another pass.

Tip: buttons inside the card—like Deep Dive and answer choices—always perform their own action; they do not navigate.
